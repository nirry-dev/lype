# Code of Conduct

Lype is a project about how people and a small, honest machine treat each other. We hold ourselves to at least the standard we ask of the letters it reads.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be welcoming and kind, assume good faith, give and accept feedback gracefully, and do not harass, demean or expose anyone.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in this project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, or letters written to a public instance of it) makes you uncomfortable, write to **info@nirry.net**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. You will get a reply.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors from the project temporarily or permanently.
